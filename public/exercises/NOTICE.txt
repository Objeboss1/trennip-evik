Exercise photos in this folder are resized copies of images from
free-exercise-db by yuhonas:
https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db

That dataset is released under the Unlicense (public domain):
https://unlicense.org

It is based on the public-domain exercises.json dataset by Ollie Jennings:
https://github.com/wrkout/exercises.json

The app shows this credit under the training plan.
